    Continuous threat modeling

    Each feature gets its own blueprint, scoped to the parts of the system it touches. Engineers threat model it while they build the feature, and its countermeasures follow the same approval routing as step 5.

    At release, the feature blueprints are merged into the product's system blueprint and Product Security signs off that version. The FDA expects a system-level threat model, and the risk assessment works on the whole product, so both use the merged version.

    A feature with no security impact gets a quick "no new threats" sign-off from the Security Champion. A feature that touches a safety-relevant damage scenario, or adds a risk, triggers a risk assessment.

    Proposed: the prototype shows a design change as "Changes since approval" on the Clinician programmer threat model, but not feature blueprints yet. CycloneDX 2.0 allows several blueprints in one TM-BOM, each with its own scope and version.
